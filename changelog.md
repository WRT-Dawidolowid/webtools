# <i class="fa-solid fa-rocket"></i> WebTools Update

## <i class="fa-solid fa-chart-pie"></i> ChartStudio (Update: V1 &rarr; V2)
A massive overhaul bringing professional-grade visualization features, a modern UI, and vastly improved data control.

### <i class="fa-solid fa-chart-column"></i> 7 New Chart Types & Visuals
* **New Types:** Added Line, Area, Doughnut, Gauge, Radar, Pyramid, and Matrix/Heatmap.
* **Heatmap Themes:** Custom color scales including Fire, Ocean, and Emerald.
* **Pro Aesthetics:** Added a pseudo-3D visual toggle for Bar/Pie/Doughnut charts and a Center Logo upload feature for Doughnut/Gauge charts.
* **Color Generation:** One-click Material Design color palette generator for your datasets.

### <i class="fa-solid fa-sliders"></i> Advanced Data Control
* **Drag-to-Reorder:** Manually sort your elements and datasets using a smooth drag-and-drop interface.
* **Visibility Toggles:** Temporarily hide or disable specific categories/groups without deleting the underlying data.
* **Smart Imports:** Import and merge multiple JSON files simultaneously, and use the new "Unload JSON" button to quickly clear the workspace.

### <i class="fa-solid fa-image"></i> Pro Export System
* **High-Res Rendering:** Improved export quality slider now properly scales `devicePixelRatio` for crystal-clear PNGs.
* **Watermarks:** Draggable workspace Watermark system (snap to corners, add author credits, or custom text).
* **Live Preview:** Movable "Export Preview Window" provides a live mini-preview of your final render.

### <i class="fa-solid fa-wand-magic-sparkles"></i> UI Overhaul
* Introduced a gorgeous **Modern UI** toggle with a dedicated Dark Mode override.
* Redesigned workspace with 50/50 smart panels for easier X-Axis and Dataset management.

## <i class="fa-solid fa-droplet"></i> RedTracker (NEW)
A privacy-first Period & Cycle Tracker designed for both personal use and clinical professionals.

### <i class="fa-solid fa-calendar-check"></i> Core Features
* **Smart Calibration:** Automatically calculates average cycle lengths and bleeding durations, or allows for manual overrides.
* **Predictive Dashboard:** Instantly view statistics, days until your expected next period, and expected ovulation dates.
* **Quick Actions:** One-click logging buttons to start or end a period for "Today" or "Yesterday".
* **Visual History:** Generate dynamic Bar/Line charts of your cycle history.

### <i class="fa-solid fa-user-doctor"></i> Modes & Reporting
* **Dual App Modes:** Switch between "Self-Tracking" (personal) and "Clinical" (for doctors managing multiple patients with ID codes).
* **Advanced Print Engine:** Generate physical PDF/Paper reports containing statistics, history tables, predictions, and custom lined spaces for handwritten notes.
* **Bilingual & Secure:** Full English (EN) and Polish (PL) language support with 100% local JSON backups.

## <i class="fa-solid fa-bolt"></i> ZenTracker (NEW)
A specialized Habit & Recovery Tracker built to monitor streaks, manage relapses, and maintain motivation.

### <i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved"></i> Habit & Recovery
* **Streak Tracking:** Set custom day goals (e.g., 90 Days) and track your Current Streak, Best Streak, and Average Streak. 
* **Dynamic Motivation:** A smart top-banner that changes its motivational quotes contextually based on your current streak length.
* **Visual Insights:** Bar and Line charts map out the days between relapses to visually show your recovery progress.

### <i class="fa-solid fa-ticket"></i> The "Pass" System
* A forgiving approach to recovery: Earn "Passes" automatically based on your average streak, or set a manual interval. 
* Use a pass to log a minor slip-up *without* resetting your main progress streak.

### <i class="fa-solid fa-language"></i> Built for Everyone
* **Multi-Profile:** Track multiple different habits or users simultaneously within the same app.
* **Bilingual & Printable:** Full EN/PL support and the ability to print physical reports of your event history, used passes, and personal recovery notes.
